# Lab 8 · A research brief you can defend (45 min)

**Goal:** a short, sourced brief produced with the Claude app, where every claim
has been traced to a source that a person opened and checked.

No code in this lab. Use the Claude app (desktop or web) with web search on.
Put your work in a Project so the brief, sources and notes stay together.

## Pick a question (choose one, or bring your own)
1. Which methods are used to separate optical crosstalk from afterpulsing in SiPM characterisation, and what does each assume?
2. For timing with SiPMs on fast scintillators, how do constant-fraction, leading-edge with walk correction, and template-fit timing compare, and under which conditions does each win?
3. What causes gain drift in SiPMs and PMTs in long runs, and how do experiments monitor and correct it?
4. What are the current options for containerising a Geant4 plus ROOT toolchain on an HPC system without root access, and what are their trade-offs?

## Steps
1. **Frame (5 min).** Write the question, why it matters to your work, and what would change your mind.
2. **Research (15 min).** Use Research mode (the "+" menu, then Research) or ordinary chat with search. Ask for sources with links.
3. **Draft (10 min).** Ask for a brief in the format of `templates/research-brief.md`, including the provenance table.
4. **Verify (15 min).** Open every source. For each claim, fill in the last two columns yourself. Mark claims that the source does not support. List any source that does not exist.

## Tiers
- **Beginner:** question 1 or 3; verify at least five claims.
- **Intermediate:** verify every claim; rewrite the short answer so it only says what survived verification.
- **Advanced:** ask a second, fresh conversation to critique the first brief against its own sources, then reconcile the two. Note where the critique was wrong.

## Acceptance
A brief with a complete provenance table, filled in by you, and a count of claims
that did not survive checking.

## Debrief question
What fraction of claims survived? Would you have caught the failures by reading the brief alone?
